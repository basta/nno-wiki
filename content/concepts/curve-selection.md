---
title: Curve selection
---

**Curve selection lemma:** if $X$ is a definable set and $x_0 \in \overline{X} \setminus X$, there exists a definable continuous curve $\gamma: (0, \varepsilon) \to X$ with $\lim_{t \to 0^+} \gamma(t) = x_0$.

Used everywhere in [[concepts/tame-geometry|tame geometry]] — e.g., to derive the [[concepts/kl-inequality|KL inequality]] and analyze limits along trajectories.
