---
title: Definable choice
---

If $S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^m \times \mathbb{R}^n$ is [[concepts/definable-sets|definable]] and $\pi: S \to \mathbb{R}^m$ is the projection, there is a **definable section** $s: \pi(S) \to \mathbb{R}^n$ with $(\pi(s(x)), s(x)) \in S$.

A workhorse: lets us pick [[concepts/definable-functions|definable]] selectors (e.g., a definable subgradient field).
