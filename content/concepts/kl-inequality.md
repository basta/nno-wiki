---
title: Kurdyka–Łojasiewicz inequality
---

For a proper lower-semicontinuous function $f$ and a critical point $x^*$, the (non-smooth) **KL inequality** asserts the existence of a concave **desingularizer** $\varphi$ such that, near $x^*$,
$$\varphi'(f(x) - f(x^*)) \cdot \operatorname{dist}(0, \partial f(x)) \ge 1.$$

**Theorem.** Every definable function in an [[concepts/o-minimal-structures|o-minimal structure]] satisfies the KL inequality at every point of its domain.

Consequences:
- Subgradient trajectories have finite length.
- Convergence rates for [[concepts/stochastic-subgradient|stochastic subgradient]] and proximal methods.
