---
title: Monotonicity theorem
---

For every definable $f: (a, b) \to \mathbb{R}$ in an [[concepts/o-minimal-structures|o-minimal structure]], there is a finite partition $a = a_0 < a_1 < \dots < a_k = b$ such that on each open subinterval $f$ is continuous and either constant, strictly increasing, or strictly decreasing.

The **$C^r$-monotonicity theorem** sharpens this to $C^r$-smoothness on each piece.
