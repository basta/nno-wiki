---
title: Partly smooth functions
---

$f$ is **partly smooth** at $x^*$ relative to a manifold $\mathcal{M}$ if:
- $f$ is smooth along $\mathcal{M}$,
- sharp in directions normal to $\mathcal{M}$,
- subdifferential continuity holds.

Stratifiable definable functions are partly smooth on each stratum; algorithms identify the active stratum in finitely many iterations under reasonable conditions ("proximal aiming").
