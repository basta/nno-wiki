---
title: Semismooth functions
---

A locally Lipschitz $f$ is **semismooth** at $x$ if the directional derivative exists and, for every sequence $x_k \to x$ along a direction $d$ and every $V_k \in \partial^\circ f(x_k)$, the limit $\lim V_k d$ equals $f'(x; d)$.

**Theorem.** Every definable, stratifiable function is semismooth.

Semismoothness underlies Newton-type methods and convergence proofs for non-smooth optimization.
