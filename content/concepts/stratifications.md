---
title: Stratifications
---

A **stratification** of a set $X$ is a locally finite partition $X = \bigsqcup_\alpha S_\alpha$ into smooth manifolds (the *strata*) with $\overline{S_\alpha} \cap S_\beta \ne \emptyset \Rightarrow S_\beta \subseteq \overline{S_\alpha}$ (frontier condition).

Stronger variants impose regularity along strata closures:
- [[concepts/whitney-stratifications|Whitney-(a), Whitney-(b)]]
- Verdier
- Thom

In [[concepts/o-minimal-structures|o-minimal structures]], every definable set admits a Whitney (and stronger) stratification.
