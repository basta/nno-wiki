---
title: Structures (model theory)
---

A **structure** $\mathcal{M} = (M, (R_i)_{i \in I}, (f_j)_{j \in J}, (c_k)_{k \in K})$ on an underlying set $M$ consists of relations, functions, and constants. **Definable** subsets of $M^n$ are those obtained by first-order formulas in the language.

For NNO, the structure of interest is an [[concepts/o-minimal-structures|o-minimal expansion]] of the ordered real field.
