---
title: VC dimension
---

The **Vapnik–Chervonenkis dimension** of a class $\mathcal{H}$ of $\{0,1\}$-valued functions is the largest $d$ such that some $d$-point set is shattered by $\mathcal{H}$.

For [[concepts/definable-functions|definable]] families in an [[concepts/o-minimal-structures|o-minimal structure]], the VC dimension is finite, and uniform convergence (a.k.a. *density*) is controlled — giving generalization bounds for neural networks via tame geometry.
