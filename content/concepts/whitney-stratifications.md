---
title: Whitney stratifications
---

A pair of strata $(S_\alpha, S_\beta)$ with $S_\beta \subseteq \overline{S_\alpha}$ satisfies:

- **Whitney-(a)** if for every sequence $x_n \in S_\alpha$ with $x_n \to y \in S_\beta$ such that $T_{x_n} S_\alpha \to \tau$, one has $T_y S_\beta \subseteq \tau$.
- **Whitney-(b)** if additionally the secant lines $\overline{x_n y_n}$ (with $y_n \in S_\beta \to y$) limit into $\tau$.

Every definable set in an [[concepts/o-minimal-structures|o-minimal structure]] admits a Whitney-(b) [[concepts/stratifications|stratification]]. This is the key ingredient behind the non-smooth [[concepts/kl-inequality|KL inequality]] and the projection formulas for the [[concepts/clarke-subdifferential|Clarke subdifferential]].
