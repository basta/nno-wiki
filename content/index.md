Personal wiki for **B4M36NNO — Non-smooth non-convex optimization for training deep neural networks** (CTU FEE, Z/2026-7).

> The "tame geometry" paradigm gives rigorous analysis of stochastic subgradient descent and related algorithms for training neural networks. Definability of activation functions in o-minimal structures lets us apply the chain rule to generalized derivatives, justify backpropagation, guarantee almost-everywhere differentiability, and rule out chaotic optimizer behavior.

## Goals

- Understand the principles behind the empirical success of neural networks.
- Analyze NN architectures and training dynamics via [[concepts/tame-geometry|tame geometry]] (definability in [[concepts/o-minimal-structures|o-minimal structures]]).
- Apply tools like [[concepts/cell-decomposition|cell decomposition]] and [[concepts/curve-selection|curve selection]] to non-smooth optimization.
- See o-minimality as a unifying language for logic, geometry, and learning.
