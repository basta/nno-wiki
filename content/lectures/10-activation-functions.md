> The "zoo": every standard deep-learning activation is definable in some o-minimal structure.

## Topics

- ReLU, leaky ReLU, GELU, SiLU, sigmoid, tanh, softplus, ELU, Swish, …
- Which o-minimal structure does each live in?
  - Piecewise-linear (ReLU): $\mathbb{R}_{\mathrm{alg}}$
  - Sigmoid, tanh, GELU, SiLU: $\mathbb{R}_{\mathrm{an,exp}}$
- Consequences: chain rule on generalized derivatives, a.e. differentiability.
